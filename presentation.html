01 / BIOLOGICAL CONTEXT

The Cell's Chief Mechanic

BRCA1 is a Tumor Suppressor Gene that monitors genetic integrity.

It initiates Homologous Recombination (HR) to repair double-strand DNA breaks caused by stress, radiation, or aging.

"A non-functional BRCA1 protein essentially leaves the genome vulnerable to catastrophic mutation accumulation."
